Privacy policy

Riftborn is a single-player ARPG with an optional local co-op prototype. We do not run player accounts or a cloud save service.

What we store on your device

Character progress, settings (volume, graphics, hotkeys, fullscreen), tutorial flags, and hub map fog are saved in the browser or Electron profile via local storage on your device. Window size/position for the desktop app may be stored in the Electron user-data folder.

We do not upload these saves to our servers.

Optional LAN co-op

Pressing P can connect to a co-op server on your local network (hub sandbox only — a prototype, not online multiplayer). Traffic stays on your LAN unless you intentionally expose that host. No account login is required.

Microsoft Store

If you install from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may collect standard Store analytics and crash telemetry under their own policies. That is separate from the game client, which does not phone home for gameplay saves.

Third-party sites

Web builds hosted on GitHub Pages or itch.io are subject to those hosts’ privacy practices. Links to GitHub or itch open in your browser.
